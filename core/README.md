## What is SENAR Core?

SENAR Core is the entry point to the SENAR methodology. It contains 8 rules that any developer can adopt immediately, without changing their team structure or processes. Think of it as the minimum viable standard.

Once you are comfortable with Core, move to the full [SENAR Standard](../standard/README.md) for team-level and enterprise-level configurations.

## Core vs Standard

- **SENAR Core** — 8 rules, individual focus, adopt in 5 minutes
- **SENAR Standard** — 15 rules, quality gates, metrics, ceremonies, team/enterprise configurations
